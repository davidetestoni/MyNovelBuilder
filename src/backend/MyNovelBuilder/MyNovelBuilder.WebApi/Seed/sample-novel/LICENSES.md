# Sample novel licensing

The title, setting, characters, compendium entries, and prose in this directory were created specifically for the MyNovelBuilder sample project. They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

No provider responses, provider metadata, private user content, credentials, or third-party fictional characters are included.

Image assets will be listed here individually when they are added. Until then, this fixture contains no media files.
